## 3. Full Description (App Store)

```text
Wroughtmark provides cryptographic proof of human craft and creative process without surrendering raw files or private intellectual property to any cloud.

PAIR & CO-SIGN WORKSTATION SESSIONS
Pair your iPhone, iPad, or Apple Watch with your Mac workstation. Co-sign active revision intervals, manuscripts, digital audio takes, and 3D renders using Apple Secure Enclave hardware key attestation (Face ID / Touch ID / Optic ID).

ADAPTIVE DESIGN FOR IPAD & EXPANDED DISPLAYS
Engineered for modern multi-window workflows, iPad Split View, Stage Manager, and next-generation adaptive folding displays. In compact views, Wroughtmark provides a focused, single-column companion. On iPad and expanded screens, the interface expands into a dual-pane Foundry Workstation, keeping your live hardware signature stream visible alongside acoustic slates and instant offline verification.

PHYSICAL STUDIO SLATES
Anchor creative takes and drafts with physical sensory witnesses:
- 5s Acoustic Room Tone: Continuous background acoustic entropy sampling verifies physical presence in a real room without recording intelligible speech.
- Handwritten Outlines & Clapper Takes: Cryptographically hash physical notebook drafts and clapperboard frames locally in Rust.
- 0 Bytes Uploaded: All hashing and signature generation happens directly on your device.

OFFLINE POCKET VERIFIER
Verify any portable Wroughtmark seal (*.seal.json) or container embedded with cryptographic process assertions (PNG iTXt, WAV WMKS, JPEG trailer). Wroughtmark verifies Ed25519 signatures, BLAKE3 digests, and external TimeAnchor receipts offline in microseconds.

APPLE PLATFORM INTEGRATION
- Secure Enclave biometric co-signing
- Apple Watch wrist companion for acoustic slates
- visionOS spatial co-signer for immersive creation
- Universal purchase across iOS and iPadOS
```
